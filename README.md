# Vector Flight: Asteroid Survival

Fly through the asteroid field, destroy asteroids, and try to stay alive while avoiding the enemy's vision.

## Controls

- Press **Left Arrow** or **A** to turn left.
- Press **Right Arrow** or **D** to turn right.
- Hold **Up Arrow** or **W** to use your engine and move forward.
- Press **Space** to shoot from the front of your ship.
- Press **R** after you die to restart.

Your ship has momentum, so it will keep drifting after you release the thrust key. The yellow line shows the direction you are moving, while the red line shows the direction your ship is facing.

Shoot an asteroid to destroy it and earn 100 points. If your ship crashes into an asteroid, the game ends and shows your final score.

The enemy slowly rotates and scans the area around it. If you are close enough and enter the direction it is facing, the enemy will turn red and detect you.
